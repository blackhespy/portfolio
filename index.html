I'm Christian Jauhari — a full-stack Data, AI & Automation practitioner based in Jakarta. I graduated from Parahyangan Catholic University with a degree in Mathematics for Data, where I developed a rigorous foundation in statistical thinking and computational problem-solving.

Since then, I've channeled that foundation into building real-world systems: from machine learning models and neural network architectures to end-to-end data pipelines and intelligent automation workflows. I don't just analyze data — I engineer solutions that organizations can act on.

My work sits at the intersection of precision and impact. Whether it's a predictive model, a live BI dashboard, or an automated business process, I build things that are robust, explainable, and built to last.
