Overview

Cyclistic is a bike-share program featuring 5,800+ bicycles and 600 docking stations. The marketing team wants to improve user conversion from casual riders to annual members. I analyzed 258,678 trips to uncover behavioral differences and provide data-driven recommendations.

Key Finding: Casual riders spend 30% more time per trip than members, but members take significantly more frequent rides. This suggests different use cases: casual riders for leisure, members for commuting.

The Challenge

The marketing team needed to understand:

  • How do casual riders and members use Cyclistic bikes differently?
  • What are the peak usage times and locations?
  • What behavioral patterns could drive conversion?

Methodology

Data Preparation

I processed 258,678 bike trip records across 13 data fields. Key steps:

  • Data Cleaning: Removed duplicate trip IDs and handled missing station data
  • Feature Engineering: Created ride length and day-of-week columns for deeper analysis
  • Grouping: Segmented rides by 10-minute intervals to identify usage patterns
  • Tools: Excel and Power Query for all processing and calculations

Key Findings

76% Member Rides
24% Casual Rides
99% Rides < 60 min

Ride Duration Analysis

  • Casual riders: Higher average ride duration (30+ minutes)
  • Members: More frequent but shorter rides (15-20 minutes average)
  • 95.3% of all rides are under 30 minutes
  • For rides under 10 minutes: 78% are members vs. 22% casual

Peak Usage Patterns

  • Peak Hour: 5 PM (8.46% of all daily rides)
  • Peak Day: Thursday (13.1% of weekly rides)
  • Highest Avg Ride Length: 2 AM Tuesday (21.25 min, 79% casual)
  • Top Station: Clinton St & Washington Blvd (near transit + tourist attractions)

Recommendations

Based on the analysis, I recommended the following marketing strategies:

  • Create a 30-Minute Pass: Target casual riders with a 10 passes/week membership for their typical 30-min leisure trips
  • Peak Hour Promotions: Run targeted campaigns 3-6 PM when usage peaks
  • Referral Program: Incentivize members to invite casual riders with rewards
  • Station Infrastructure: Expand facilities at high-traffic stations near public transit and attractions
  • Weekend Passes: Create special pricing for Sunday/Monday (lowest usage days) to boost engagement

Impact

Deliverables: Comprehensive data visualizations, trend analysis report, and actionable marketing recommendations—providing clear direction for membership conversion strategy based on real user behavior data.
