Overview

This project develops artificial neural network (ANN) models to accelerate Asian option pricing in financial derivatives. Traditional methods like Monte Carlo are computationally expensive; I built a data-driven approach that achieves 10-100x speedup while maintaining high accuracy.

Impact: ANN models reduce computation time from seconds (Monte Carlo) to milliseconds, enabling real-time pricing and risk analysis in high-frequency trading environments.

The Problem

Asian options are complex financial derivatives where payoff depends on the average asset price over time. Pricing them is computationally challenging:

  • No closed-form analytical solutions exist
  • Numerical methods (Monte Carlo) require 10,000+ simulations per price
  • Computation time prohibits real-time pricing at scale
  • Financial firms need faster, more efficient alternatives

Technical Approach

Data Generation

I generated training data using three established pricing methods:

  • Levy's Approximation: Direct analytical approach using lognormal distribution matching
  • Monte Carlo with Antithetic Variables: Variance reduction through antithetic sampling
  • Quasi-Monte Carlo (Sobol): Low-discrepancy sequences for better distribution coverage

Neural Network Architecture

After extensive hyperparameter tuning, optimal ANN configuration:

  • Layers: 2 hidden layers, 50 neurons each
  • Activation: ReLU (Rectified Linear Unit)
  • Optimizer: ADAM (Adaptive Moment Estimation)
  • Loss Function: Mean Squared Error (MSE)
  • Training: 80% data, 20% validation with early stopping (patience=5)

Model Training

The network learns to map option parameters (spot price, strike, volatility, time-to-maturity) to fair option price. Training process:

  • Data standardization to mean=0, std=1
  • Backpropagation across epochs
  • Early stopping prevents overfitting
  • Validation accuracy monitored throughout

Key Insights

  • Model Quality: Optimal hyperparameters yield small MSE with R² close to 1.0
  • Scalability: ANN approach scales linearly, Monte Carlo scales quadratically
  • Practical Use: Real-time pricing now feasible for portfolios with thousands of positions
  • Trade-off: Training takes time upfront, but inference cost is negligible

Recommendations & Future Work

  • Production Deployment: Integrate into pricing engine using TensorFlow/ONNX for cross-platform compatibility
  • Model Ensembling: Combine multiple ANN architectures to reduce variance
  • Extended Coverage: Train separate models for different option types and regimes
  • Real-time Monitoring: Track model drift and retrain periodically with fresh market data
  • Explainability: Use SHAP or LIME to explain predictions for risk management compliance
